CMSC 828A provides an in-depth tour of machine learning paradigms — their assumptions, formulations, motivations, where to use them, and the ideas connecting them. The course follows three routes: (1) supervised → semi-supervised → unsupervised and self-supervised → online and reinforcement learning; (2) single-task → transfer → multi-task → meta-learning; (3) single-model → adversarial → ensemble → federated → multi-agent learning. I developed the whole course, project topics, and reading list from scratch.
