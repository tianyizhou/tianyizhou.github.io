I lead the **Tianyi Lab** at the University of Maryland. We study how machines can learn the way strong learners do: choosing *what* to learn and *when* (curricula, data selection, test-time compute), combining specialists into generalists (mixture-of-experts, collaborative and federated learning), and staying grounded in the world (neuro-symbolic world models, interactive agents). Our current work spans **LLM reasoning, multimodal and unified models, agents, and human–AI alignment**, built on recent LLMs, unified multimodal models, RL and agentic systems, with applications in education, science, design, visualization and embodied intelligence.

Representative projects include [Program-of-Layers](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.06574), [ThinkARM](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1513.pdf), [HallusionBench](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Guan_HallusionBench_An_Advanced_Diagnostic_Suite_for_Entangled_Language_Hallucination_and_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf), [WALL-E](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.15785), [MoE-Embedding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10814), [BLIP3-o](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.09568), [SuperFiltering](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.00530), [InstructZero](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.03082), [FedProto](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.00243.pdf), [Robust Curriculum Learning](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=lmTWnm3coJJ), [Curriculum HER](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2019/file/83715fd4755b33f9c3958e1a9ee221e1-Paper.pdf), [DiSAN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.04696.pdf), [Divide-and-Conquer Anchoring](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.5752) and [GoDec](https://icml.cc/2011/papers/41_icmlpaper.pdf). Our work appears at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLM, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, KDD, AAAI and IJCAI, and in journals including TPAMI, TMLR and *Machine Learning*.

#### Research themes

- **Human–AI–World alignment** — cognitive and epistemic alignment, human modeling, multi-objective alignment; neuro-symbolic and physics/geometry-grounded world models; interactive agents.
- **Self-evolving AI and sustainable learning** — data/task/environment selection, synthesis and curricula; continual learning; auto-benchmarking and auditing; self-reward and self-labeling.
- **Generalist–specialist cycle** — dynamic models, mixture-of-experts, federated and collaborative learning, multi-agent orchestration.
- **Interpretability and foundations of ML** — memorization, generalization, grokking, collapse, representation, hallucination and steering.
