We release [BLIP3-o](https://github.com/JiuhaiChen/BLIP3o), a fully-open sourced framework to unify image reasoning + generation in one model. It aligns the two fundamental tasks via CLIP-level features. You can find [report](https://huggingface.co/papers/2505.09568) and [demo](https://t.co/dbv6xed2X5).
