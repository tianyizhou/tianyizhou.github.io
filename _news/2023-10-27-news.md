Data recycling and filtering improves instruction-tuning of LLMs, leading to recycled LLMs outperforming other larger LLMs trained on new data and RLHF. We release [Reflection-Tuning preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11716), [codebase](https://github.com/tianyi-lab/Reflection_Tuning), and the [model](https://huggingface.co/umd-zhou-lab/recycled-wizardlm-7b-v2.0).
