We release [ATLaS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02197) and [EEF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13145) that study better usage of expert trajectories in supervised/reinforcement finetuning of LLM agents. ATLaS selects only 30% critical steps from expert trajectories to achieve better generalization across tasks/envs, while EEF show that expert failed trajectories can considerably improve the agent exploration in RL.
