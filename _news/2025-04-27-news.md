We release [ColorBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/umd-zhou-lab/ColorBench), the first comprehensive benchmark exposing the weaknesses of existing VLMs on [color perception, reasoning, and robustness](https://x.com/zhoutianyi/status/1912922850136121571), across >5,800 images from 11 tasks based on real applications including shopping, medical test-kit reading, map-reading, agriculture, art, wildlife research, etc.
