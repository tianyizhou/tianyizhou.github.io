We release two works investigating how training data for LLMs ([slow vs. fast thinking](https://huggingface.co/papers/2410.23743), [instruction vs. reasoning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2504.10766)) lead to different layer-wise gradient patterns and training dynamics, from which we unified various existing data quality metrics into one: small but diverse gradient directions indicate better data.
