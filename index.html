Tianyi Zhou

Towards AI that automatically pushes its learning frontier, turns experience into reusable expertise, cycles between generalist and specialist, explores the unknown, and stays aligned with humans and the world.

Visiting Faculty @ MBZUAI · Prev. Assistant Professor @ UMD CS & Research Scientist @ Google · Ph.D. UW CS

Tianyi Lab @ GitHub

🤗 umd-zhou-lab @ HF

I lead the Tianyi Lab, where we study how machines can learn the way strong learners do in an open world: choosing what to learn and when, cycling between generalists and specialists, and staying aligned with humans and the world. Our current work spans LLM reasoning, unified multimodal models, agents, and human–AI–world alignment, with applications in education, science, design, and digital/embodied agents.

Recent recognition includes the ACL 2026 SAC Highlight Award and the 2026 eAA Best Research Award (more on the CV). I have advised 14 Ph.D. students, including a 2026 Apple Scholar in AI/ML (Ming Li) and a 2025 UMD Flagship Fellow (Dongping Chen); the group page has the full list.
